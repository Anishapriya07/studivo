Welcome to Studivo Admin 👋

As an administrator, you manage the central Knowledge Hub. Your role is to review and moderate student contributions to ensure a high-quality, curated library for all learners.

🛠️ Support

  • Guidelines: Always verify factual accuracy and formatting before approving summaries.
  • Help / Contact Information: For technical escalation, reach out to support@studivo.admin.
  • Basic Support Instructions: Use the navigation menu to view student requests, add new curated books, or approve pending student books. If an issue arises, check the browser console or clear localStorage.

📜 Terms & Conditions

  • Content should be appropriate for students.
  • Admin reviews submitted books before publishing.
  • Student requests can be approved or deleted.
  • Only approved books appear in the public Knowledge Library.
